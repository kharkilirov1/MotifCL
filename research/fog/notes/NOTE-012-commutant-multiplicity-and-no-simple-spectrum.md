# NOTE-012 — multiplicity is visible in the commutant even when spectra cannot fix gauge

Let `rho` be an absolutely irreducible real representation of dimension `b`
and consider `m` equivalent copies:

\[
V \cong \mathbb R^m \otimes \mathbb R^b,
\qquad
\pi(g)=I_m\otimes \rho(g).
\]

By the real Schur/double-centralizer picture for this absolutely irreducible
case,

\[
\operatorname{End}_G(V)\cong M_m(\mathbb R),
\]

so the commutant has dimension

\[
\boxed{m^2}.
\]

Meanwhile every element generated by the representation algebra has the form

\[
I_m\otimes X,
\]

and therefore every eigenvalue of `X` appears with multiplicity at least `m`.
Thus a simple-spectrum anchor is impossible whenever `m>1`.

A generic symmetric commutant element instead has the form

\[
H_m\otimes I_b.
\]

If `H_m` has distinct eigenvalues, each is repeated exactly `b` times and its
eigenspaces recover the `m` invariant copies.  The basis *inside* each copy is
still free; solving the joint intertwiner equations aligns those copies.

This is the algebraic reason EXP-031 succeeds where a single-eigenbasis compiler
must fail.
